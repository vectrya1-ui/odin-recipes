Lemon Pasta

Description

Lemon Pasta is a bright, silky, and refreshing dish made with pasta tossed in a light sauce of fresh lemon juice, zest, garlic, butter or olive oil, and Parmesan cheese. For a delicious and southern italian kick, you can add some chili flakes. Its simple ingredients create a vibrant balance of creamy, tangy, and savory flavors, making it perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or an elegant summer meal.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces of pasta: Opt for a thin long pasta. Fresh pasta would give the optimal experience and isn't all that time consuming. A recipe for homemade pasta can be found here
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 6 large garlic cloves, minced
  • (Optional)1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 cup parsley
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta.

    Bring a large pot of water to a boil, then salt it well. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions to al dente (about 8 minutes). Reserve 1 cup of the pasta cooking water before draining.

  2. Make a simple lemon-garlic olive oil sauce.

    When the pasta is nearly done (about 5 minutes into cooking it), heat 1/4 cup of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes, and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Whisk in the lemon juice and about 1/4 cup of the pasta cooking water, saving the remaining water for later.

  3. Coat the pasta in the oil.

    Drain the pasta, then add it to the skillet. Toss over medium heat until it’s well-coated.

  4. Finish and serve.

    Remove the skillet from the heat and add the parsley, lemon zest and parmesan (if using). Season with salt and pepper to taste. Toss again to combine, and if needed, add a little bit more of the pasta cooking water and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. Serve immediately!

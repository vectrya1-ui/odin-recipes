Cacio e Pepe

Description

Cacio e Pepe (pronounced ca-cho ee pepe) is a Roman pasta dish that translates to “cheese and pepper”. The simple dish consists of spaghetti, black pepper and Pecorino Romano (and in our case a little butter too!)

Ingredients

  • Bucatini (similar to spaghetti, except slightly thicker and is in the form of a tube).
  • Pecorino Romano
  • Black pepper
  • Unsalted butter

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and generously season with salt. Add pasta and cook until al dente.
  2. Reserve some pasta water and cool slightly.
  3. Drain pasta in a colander.
  4. Place skillet (or same pot used to cook pasta) over medium heat and add pasta back into pot.
  5. Add reserved pasta water and butter.
  6. Toss together.
  7. Add grated cheese and black pepper and continue to toss together until sauce comes together and is smooth and creamy.
  8. Top with more grated cheese and serve.
